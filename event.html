Campus Game Night

Category: Social

Date and Time: September 18, 2026 at 7:00 PM

Location: Student Center

Campus Game Night gives students a chance to relax, meet new people, and enjoy an evening of games, music, food, and friendly competition. Students of all experience levels are welcome.

About the Event

Campus Game Night brings students together for an evening of entertainment and community. A variety of board games, card games, and group activities will be available throughout the night.

What to Expect

  • A variety of board games and card games
  • Team and individual activities
  • Music, snacks, and refreshments
  • Opportunities to meet other students

Event Schedule

  • 7:00 PM: Doors open and student check-in
  • 7:15 PM: Welcome and event introduction
  • 7:30 PM: Games and activities begin
  • 9:30 PM: Final games and prize announcements
  • 10:00 PM: Event ends

Accessibility and Participation

The Student Center is wheelchair accessible. Students who need additional accommodations should contact the event organizer before the event. Participation in all games and activities is optional.

Event Information

Date: September 18, 2026

Time: 7:00 PM - 10:00 PM

Location: Esports Arena

Organizer: Office of Student Engagement

Admission: Free for students
